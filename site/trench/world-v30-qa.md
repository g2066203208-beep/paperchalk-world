# V30 old commercial quarter

Reference: https://caen.fr/index.php/annuaire-equipement/maisons-de-la-rue-saint-pierre ; https://www.cadomus.org/articles/ville-de-caen-a-coeur-de-pierre ; https://caen1936.fr/sources/ . Fictional Normandy street, not an exact reconstruction.

Actual generator + shared triangulator: 240 buildings, 175 shops, 3 bridges, 61,051 parts, 610,585 raw triangles, 24 degenerate (skipped by world-region emitter), all finite. 261 center-line samples x=-140..-10 z=96 clear of city.colliders with .28m margin. Not a full character physics test. Atlas 786432 bytes. 11,436 face parts; 1,216 polygon parts. Three software-raster views from actual geometry and atlas archived as world-v30-street-review.png. These do not reproduce the browser GPU lighting. GPU FPS, walking GIF, complete route and player collision remain unverified. Prior trench/bridge coordinates retained.
