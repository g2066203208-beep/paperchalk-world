# V19 low-poly hair cards

References: https://3d.crdg.jp/tech/archives/844 (large silhouette blocks, board polygons); https://zeroshina.jp/artwork/lowpoly-256fes-2023/ (strict low-poly example); https://www.ucbugg.com/labs/modeling/polygon-hair-modeling (swept ponytail flow). Source art unchanged. Seven folded swept strips, two fringe strips, two tail strips. 114 quads, 204 vertices, one RGB 185,170,144. No external shell, no textures. Four-view and 32-frame CPU turntable. Artistic fidelity and coverage not final, browser deployment unverified.
