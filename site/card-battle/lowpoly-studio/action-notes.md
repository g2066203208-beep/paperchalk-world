# V46 action library 03

38 procedural keyframed clips on the 506-quad V2 mesh. Full root pitch/yaw, horizontal/vertical displacement, torso lean/twist, bilateral shoulder/elbow/wrist/hip/knee/ankle. Includes 38 movement/combat/traversal test clips. The root translation is in mesh coordinates, not camera animation. Stage guides are rendered without contact or occlusion; no planted-foot IK, grip, obstacle collision, weapon attachment, cloth/hair deformation, or production skeleton export. Some poses are stylized stress tests, not polished game animations. The initial test exposed hip/axilla folds; per-pose bounded local geometry correction uses intraquad normal-angle gradient, up to 0.065 model units displacement. This is a geometric corrective, not FEM. 61 evenly spaced samples per clip, 2318 total, zero detected strict nonadjacent noncoplanar intersections, negative intraquad normal-angle warnings or zero-area triangles. Excludes shared-vertex triangle pairs, coplanar contacts and continuous time; no guarantee outside sampled poses. V46 frozen, no topology change. Further work: physical contact IK for climbing and locomotion, temporal smoothness of corrective solver, garment motion. Preview rows: jump, crouch, turn, slash, wall climb, rope climb; four samples per row.

Revision: larger alternate upward reach for climbing, hanging and jump preparation, and actual lateral leg spread for strafing/dodging. All 2318 samples rechecked after changes.
